## Why This Site Exists

Most English-language guides to Japan cover the same dozen cities in the same order: Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, maybe Hiroshima if there's time. That's not wrong, exactly — those places are worth visiting — but it leaves out most of the country. Somewhere around [X years] of traveling through Japan's smaller cities and rural regions, I got tired of guides that either ignored these places entirely or reduced them to a single paragraph copied from a tourism board press release.

This site is the guide I wished existed: specific, honest, and written by someone who actually went to the places being described.

## What Makes a Guide Here Different

Every destination covered on this site has actually been visited — not researched secondhand from press releases and other blogs. That means:

- **Real costs**, in yen, based on what things actually cost at the time of writing — not vague ranges copied from official brochures
- **Honest logistics** — how long the train actually takes, what the ferry schedule is actually like, what happens when your plan doesn't work out
- **The awkward parts included** — if I got the tasting order wrong, missed a connection, or a plan fell apart, that's in the article, because that's more useful than pretending every trip goes perfectly

## How This Site Is Maintained

Prices, opening hours, and transport schedules change. When something in an older article is reported as outdated — by a reader, or by a return visit — the article gets corrected, not left to quietly go stale. If you spot something that's changed, the [Contact page](/contact/) is the fastest way to flag it.

## Languages

This site is written primarily for English-speaking travelers, with translated guides also available in Korean (한국어) and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).

## A Note on How This Site Makes Money
